Executive Summary

Travel insurance is experiencing its biggest search demand surge since COVID, driven by the Iran-US/Israel conflict, Middle East airspace closures, and a record-breaking wave of global flight disruptions. The dominant theme is a massive coverage gap: standard policies exclude war and military action, leaving millions of travelers unprotected. CFAR (Cancel For Any Reason) queries have surged 27% in March alone, and Squaremouth reports an 18x spike in UAE/Dubai-related insurance inquiries. Wego should prioritize crisis-explainer content around war exclusions, CFAR guides, and Middle East-specific insurance advice — these are high-intent, high-volume queries where early content will dominate rankings.
